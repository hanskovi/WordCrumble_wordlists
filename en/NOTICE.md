# English word list (EN)

**Source:** ENABLE (Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon), the master word list `enable1.txt`,
as distributed via https://github.com/dolph/dictionary

https://sources.debian.org/src/scowl/6-2/r/enable/README)

## Changes by ConceptCraft (2026-10-12)

- Removed by hand: hm (2026-10-14), sri (2026-10-16).
